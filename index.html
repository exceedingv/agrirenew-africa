Our story

We didn't start on a whiteboard. We started on the farm.

For over twelve years, before AgriRenew had a name, our team was in the field — planting cereals and crops, raising livestock and fish, cultivating vegetables across hectares in different parts of Nigeria. We learned what breaks smallholder farming from the inside: energy is expensive, credit is out of reach, good training is rare, and markets don't reward the people who grow the food.

So we built a company that fixes all four at once. Waste-to-energy was our first engine — backed by a Development Bank of Nigeria equipment grant — and around it we grew an academy, a farmer credit system, a verified marketplace, and a climate advisory practice. Each part feeds the others.

"We don't just talk about renewing African agriculture. We've done it, we're doing it, and we can prove it."

Farmer Credit & Inputs Program

Every registered farmer gets an AgriRenew account. Supplying farm waste builds credit — and credit unlocks real support, priced by the volume each farmer contributes.

  • Briquettes at below-market prices for participating farmers
  • Input loans: fertilizers, seeds, and farm equipment at affordable rates
  • Support with land acquisition for expanding farmers
  • Forward purchase: we buy produce early at agreed prices, protecting farmers from market swings
  • Free registration, ongoing training, and waste-handling support

Climate action, built in

Every briquette is a climate solution

We don't bolt climate action onto our business — it is the business. Diverting farm waste from open burning, replacing charcoal and firewood, and keeping trees standing: that's what our loop does every single day.

Waste diverted, emissions avoided

Crop residues that would be burned in the open or left to rot become clean cooking fuel instead — cutting smoke, methane, and haze at the source.

Forests kept standing

Every household that cooks with our bio-briquettes is a household not buying charcoal — directly reducing the pressure that drives deforestation across the region.

Climate-smart design for others

We design and advise on climate solutions for governments and businesses — bringing field-tested, Africa-first methods to agriculture programs beyond our own.

The farmer partnership

How the credit program works

A real sequence, not a slogan. Waste goes in on day one — value keeps coming back for as long as you farm with us.

Register free

Sign up for the waste-to-energy program. We open your AgriRenew farmer account and train you on collecting and preserving farm waste.

Supply your waste

Deliver crop residues and farm waste from your fields. Every delivery is logged to your account and becomes briquette feedstock.

Build credit

Your delivery volume sets your credit level — and your eligibility for input loans, equipment, and land support.

Grow & sell back

Buy briquettes below market price, take input loans, and sell produce to us early at protected prices — or list it on the marketplace.
