    Our story

    We didn't start on a whiteboard. We started on the farm.

    For over twelve years, before AgriRenew had a name, our team was in the field: planting cereals and crops, raising livestock and fish, and growing vegetables across hectares in different parts of Nigeria. We saw the same four problems everywhere.

    1. Waste burned in the open while households paid more every month for charcoal.
    2. Farmers locked out of credit because they had no records a lender would accept.
    3. Buyers unable to trust where produce came from or how it was grown.
    4. Young people who wanted to farm but had no practical, business-first training.

    So we built one company that fixes all four at once. Waste-to-energy was our first engine, backed by a Development Bank of Nigeria equipment grant. Around it we grew an academy, a farmer credit system, a verified marketplace, climate advisory and community programmes.

    "We don't just talk about renewing African agriculture. We've done it, we're doing it, and we can prove it."

      What we grow and support

      Real crops, grown clean

      We work with food crops, vegetables, livestock and fish, using certified non-GMO seed, healthy soil and as few chemicals as the crop needs.

      Climate action, built in

      Every briquette is a climate solution

      Diverting farm waste from open burning, replacing charcoal and firewood, and keeping trees standing: our loop does this every day.

      Waste diverted, emissions avoided

      Crop residues that would be burned in the open become clean cooking fuel instead, cutting smoke and haze at the source.

      Forests kept standing

      Every household cooking with our bio-briquettes is not buying charcoal, which reduces the pressure behind deforestation.

      The farmer partnership

      How the credit programme works

      Waste goes in on day one, and value keeps coming back for as long as you farm with us.

      1. Register free

        We open your AgriRenew farmer account and train you to collect and store farm waste.

      2. Supply your waste

        Deliver crop residues and farm waste. Every delivery is logged to your account.

      3. Build credit

        Your delivery volume sets your credit level and your eligibility for input loans, equipment and land support.

      4. Grow & sell

        Buy briquettes below market price, take input loans, and sell produce to us early at protected prices or list it on the marketplace.

      Questions, answered

      Frequently asked questions

      Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

      Is AgriRenew Africa only a briquette company?

      No. Waste-to-energy is one division inside a full ecosystem that also includes consultancy, the AgriRenew Academy, a farmer credit and inputs programme, a verified produce marketplace, climate advisory and community programmes.

      Can AgriRenew implement government or donor-funded programmes?

      Yes. We act as implementing partner for farmer registration, training, input distribution, waste-to-energy and climate-smart agriculture projects, and we report against your programme indicators. Contact us to request our capability statement.

      How does AgriRenew fight climate change?

      Three ways at once: we divert farm waste from open burning, our briquettes replace charcoal and firewood, and we design climate-smart agriculture solutions for governments and businesses.

      Do you use GMO seeds?

      No. We work with certified non-GMO seed, and we promote healthy soil, crop rotation and careful use of fertilisers and crop protection.

      How do I join the farmer credit programme?

      Registration is free. Contact us to open your farmer account, get trained on waste collection and start supplying farm waste. Your delivery volume builds your credit level.

      What does the Academy teach?

      Crop production and soil science, livestock, poultry and fish farming, drones and AI in agriculture, agribusiness and export readiness. Every course ends with a practical project.

      Can buyers trust the marketplace?

      Yes. Every farmer is verified, their production details are recorded, and they are licensed by AgriRenew before they can list.

      Do you work outside Nigeria?

      Yes. AgriRenew is built for Africa. We design agriculture and climate solutions for partners beyond Nigeria and help producers get licensed to export.
