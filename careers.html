How we hire

Five steps, usually within three weeks

  1. ApplySend your details and CV. We read every application.
  2. Intro callA 20-minute call about you, the role and your questions.
  3. Practical taskA short task or field visit that looks like the real job.
  4. Meet the foundersA conversation about values and how you like to work.
  5. OfferWe make a decision quickly and tell every candidate the outcome.

Interns, graduates & NYSC corps members

Every year we host interns, industrial-training students and NYSC corps members in field operations, production, the Academy and digital media.

Questions

Candidate FAQ

Do I need an agriculture degree?

Not for most roles. We value field experience, curiosity and reliability. Each role lists what it actually needs.

Where will I work?

Our base is in Abuja. Field roles travel to partner farms and communities; some roles are hybrid.

How do I send my CV?

Fill in the form below and select "Prepare my application". Your email app opens with the details ready: attach your CV there and send.

I don't see a role that fits. Can I still apply?

Yes. Choose "General application" and tell us what you would like to do. We contact our talent pool first when new roles open.
