How every engineer is vetted

  1. Profile and portfolio reviewShipped work, public code and references.
  2. Technical assessmentA take-home task in the candidate's own stack, reviewed by a senior engineer.
  3. Live pairing session90 minutes solving a real problem together, including code review and debugging.
  4. Communication checkWritten and spoken English, async updates, and working with product owners.
  5. Reference and background checksPast employers, identity, and NYSC or education records.
  6. Two-week trial with youIf the fit is wrong, we replace the engineer at no cost.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

Something else on your mind? Send us a message and a technical lead will reply within one working day.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a free discovery call we send a written estimate with a fixed price or a monthly team rate. We price in naira for Nigerian clients and in USD or GBP for international clients.

How quickly can engineers start?

We usually send a shortlist within five working days. Once you pick an engineer, they can start as soon as contracts are signed, often within two weeks.

Who owns the code?

You do. All source code, designs and documentation are assigned to you under the contract, and they live in your own repositories from the first commit.

What if an engineer isn't the right fit?

Every placement starts with a two-week trial. If it isn't working, we replace the engineer at no extra cost.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Our hours overlap fully with the UK and Europe and partly with the US East Coast. We use your tools: Slack or Teams, Jira or Linear, GitHub or GitLab.

Who can join the Tesler Academy?

Anyone aged 18 or over who passes the aptitude test. You don't need a computer science degree. You need a laptop, an internet connection and about 20 hours a week.

How do Academy classes run?

Online tracks run live in the evenings and on Saturdays (WAT). Hybrid tracks add in-person lab days. Every learner gets weekly code reviews from a working engineer.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a one-to-two week code and infrastructure audit, fix the urgent risks first, and then continue development.
